# Main-figure composition and whitespace

This is the main-figure owner of composition decisions. Generic registration
belongs to figure-layout-protocol.md; measured text and port implementation
belongs to measured-layout.md. Do not copy these rules into motivation figures.

## Compose from the mechanism

Preserve a comfortable macro board when the user likes it. Give each region a
clear entry, local process and exit; size regions from readable contents, not
from a fixed number of equal columns. Comparable objects share rails, sizes
and baselines. Different scientific roles may have different local layouts.

Use supported objects to explain transformations. A document should show the
relevant sentence or edit; state selection should visibly select a state;
equal weighting should use equal marks or a concise sourced label. Decorative
mini-networks, identical unexplained bars and prose cards do not add information.

Draw the geometry before refining captions. If removing labels reveals only
empty shells, revisit the representation. This is a diagnostic, not a rule
that every scientific object needs a complicated icon.

## Align relationships

Declare the actual relation: common left/right edges, row baseline, equal
cell size, body centering, or a deliberate optical alignment. A text anchor
alone is insufficient. Use the component helpers to derive positions and
ports together. Do not manually round each item to a different coordinate.

Whitespace has several valid roles: internal padding, separation of concepts,
hierarchy and routing. Inspect accidental pockets alongside crowding. Prefer
moving or resizing supported content before adding detail. Do not stretch a
module, add scientific content or shrink type solely to close an empty patch.

## Use occupancy as a diagnostic

Schema-2 panel specs still register one panel_occupancy check per panel so its
span and largest empty region are computed. With measured-v1, the check may be
SOFT: record ADJUSTED when it passes or KEPT_WITH_REASON with the actual grouping
or routing purpose of the identified empty region. The numerical thresholds
remain comparable to historical audits; they are not universal design targets.

The measured-v1 profile keeps text containment and intended alignment hard.
Density cannot excuse collisions or missing information. Existing non-measured
profiles retain their historical hard occupancy behavior and are not new
PAPER_READY evidence.

The occupancy calculation is only a bounding-box diagnostic. Labels, formulas,
backgrounds and invisible geometry cannot supply occupied scientific objects.
A large outlined box may hide an empty interior; inspect the wordless render
and local crops even after a numerical PASS. Do not optimize the score itself.

## Acceptance

At final size, verify the reading path, consistent gutters, the distinction
between macro and micro objects, and retention against the source/reference.
Useful detail stays legible. A simple honest method need not imitate the density
of a much larger industrial system. Visual acceptance remains a separate
judgment from geometric compliance.

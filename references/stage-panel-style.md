# Stage-panel styling

Use stages only when the verified graph has stages. Keep a comfortable board
and choose widths from content and scientific importance. Composition and
occupancy policy belong to composition-grid-and-whitespace.md.

Repeated tags, modules and headers share their applicable edge, baseline,
padding and typography. Different stages may use different internal layouts.
Use figure_components.py for measured frames, text, tag rows and ports; see
measured-layout.md for the exact profile.

Use light backgrounds, neutral prose and a restrained palette with named
scientific roles. Preserve each role across panels. Stage tints must not
change the meaning of class or selection colors. Hollow/filled marks, labels
and line styles preserve meaning without color alone.

Ordinary data arrows are short, neutral and straight or single-elbow where
practical. Keep their salience below the scientific objects; do not prescribe
one line-width ratio for every figure. Graph relations are not ordinary
pipeline arrows. Necessary feedback paths need a source-supported meaning,
a clear return port and a short label or dash pattern.

Close icon contours and inspect their actual exports. A folded-page glyph
should not depend on a renderer accidentally drawing an omitted edge.
Avoid ornamental controls, redundant badges, excessive border nesting and
slash-separated prose. Do not enlarge titles or add prose to fill a void.

For exact type sizes use the venue placement. Essential labels default to
7 pt or the venue's stronger requirement. No silent shrinking; reflow, resize
or shorten source-faithful wording. Match font roles across repeated modules.
